Questions

Good to know.

Which email providers can I connect?

Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo, iCloud, and custom mailboxes that support IMAP and SMTP. Proton Mail, Tuta, and HEY Mail cannot be connected because they block third-party IMAP and SMTP access.

Is OneInbox safe?

Your password is encrypted before it is saved. We keep your mailbox data private and do not sell it or use it to train AI.
