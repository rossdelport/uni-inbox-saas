Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 August 2026

OneInbox shows all your mailboxes in one place, so your privacy is the product. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the rules we hold ourselves to. It sits alongside our Terms of Service.

1. Information we collect

We collect different types of information to provide and improve the service:

  • Personal information: name, email address, phone number, or other details you provide when signing up or using the service.
  • Mail data: when you connect a mailbox, we sync a rolling window of recent messages so we can show them in your unified inbox and send the replies you write. Your originals always remain in your mailbox at your provider.
  • Usage data: information about how you access and use the service, including IP address, browser type, pages visited, and time spent on pages.
  • Cookies and tracking data: cookies or similar technologies to track activity and improve the user experience.

2. How we use your information

We use the data we collect to provide and maintain the service, send updates or announcements, analyze usage to improve features and performance, and ensure security while preventing unauthorized access.

3. Data sharing and disclosure

Your data is never sold or rented.

We may share information with trusted service providers who help us operate the service, such as hosting or analytics platforms. We may also disclose information when required by law or in response to legal requests, or during business transfers like mergers or acquisitions.

4. Google API Services and your Gmail data

If you connect a Gmail account, OneInbox accesses your Gmail data through Google API Services solely to provide the features you use: showing your mail inside your unified inbox, keeping read and archive state in sync, and sending the replies you write.

OneInbox's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

In plain terms: we never sell your Gmail data, never use it for advertising, and never let humans read it, except with your explicit permission, for security purposes, or where required by law. If you ask for an AI summary, that conversation is sent to our AI provider only to generate the summary and is not used to train AI models. Synced Gmail messages follow the retention window described below, and disconnecting your account removes its synced mail from OneInbox.

5. Data retention

We keep your personal information only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy or as required by law. Synced mail is kept on a rolling window per connected account, and disconnecting an account removes its synced mail from OneInbox.

Once data is no longer needed, it is securely deleted or anonymized.

6. Your data protection rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, update, or delete your personal information, withdraw consent for processing, or file a complaint with a data protection authority.

To exercise these rights, contact us at ross@tryoneinbox.co.

7. Changes to this privacy policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page, along with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
